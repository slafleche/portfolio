I began as a **themer**, building client themes inside Vanilla's existing
framework. That work exposed me to how the system actually behaved: where it
flexed, where it resisted, and how design intent carried through to code. I
often had to balance two conflicting pressures: clients who wanted full creative
freedom, and my lead, who emphasized restraint to keep refactors safe. I
naturally found myself bridging those needs, finding solutions that respected
both the visual goals and the system's structural limits.
