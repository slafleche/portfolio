I started in animation and 3D modeling—building objects that had to match both
artistic vision and technical constraints. That taught me to see the systems
beneath the surface.

When I moved to front-end development, I gravitated toward the infrastructure:
theming, tokens, component APIs. I've built systems that serve multiple products
while keeping design coherent and development maintainable. I think about
developer experience the way designers think about user experience—clear systems
make good ideas easier to ship.
