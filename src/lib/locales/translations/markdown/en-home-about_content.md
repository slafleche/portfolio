I started in animation and 3D modeling, working on objects that had to satisfy
both artistic vision and technical constraints. That experience taught me to
look for the systems beneath the surface.

When I moved to front-end development, I gravitated toward the infrastructure:
theming, tokens, component [abbr:API]s. I've built systems that serve multiple
products while keeping design coherent and development maintainable. I think
about developer experience the way designers think about user experience. Clear
systems make good ideas easier to ship.
